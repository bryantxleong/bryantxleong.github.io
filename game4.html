Game Intro

“Swiss or Miss” is a simulation game where players take on the role of a graphic designer.

Players must complete design tasks based on their clients' requirements while also managing their stress levels.

At the end of each day, the player's work is evaluated based on whether it meets the client's requirements.

This project was my first group project and was developed using Unity and C#, giving me experience in working with a team throughout the game development process.

Challenging

1. Underestimating Art in Games

During the early stage of development, as I was not part of the art team, I often overlooked their requirements and had very little communication with them. I also did not fully understand the importance of maintaining a consistent art direction.

The game was intended to strongly reflect the Swiss Style, but I did not fully understand what that meant visually at the time. As a result, the first version of the game was developed with an inconsistent visual style.

Solution

I cannot say that I have fully solved this problem yet. However, I have kept this experience in mind and continue to develop a deeper understanding of art and its importance in game development.

2. Game Integration

As this was my first group project, each programmer initially developed their features as independent systems. At the time, I overestimated my ability to integrate these systems later in development.

I did not expect how different each programmer’s coding style and structure could be. When I needed to work with or modify another member’s features, these differences caused many unexpected bugs and made the integration much more difficult.

Solution

Rather than having a single solution, I relied on communicating with my teammates whenever integration issues appeared.

This experience taught me that a team needs proper planning before development begins, including agreeing on coding conventions, code structure, and design patterns before working on the project.
